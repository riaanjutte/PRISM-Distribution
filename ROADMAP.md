# PRISM public roadmap

PRISM is under active development. This roadmap highlights the features and improvements currently being considered. Priorities may change in response to testing and user feedback, and no release dates are promised.

## Current priorities

- Improve lighting and exposure consistency between the interactive viewport and Blender/Cycles renders, especially inside cockpits and hangars.
- Continue improving reliability, performance and compatibility across the Great Battles and Korea aircraft libraries.
- Validate automatic updating on clean user installations.

## Planned next

- **Aircraft loadouts:** Display appropriate fuel tanks, bombs, rockets, pylons and racks using locally installed game assets.
- **Saved camera views and multi-angle export:** Save named views such as port, starboard, underside and detail shots, then render consistent image sets across multiple liveries.
- **Before-and-after skin comparison:** Compare two livery revisions with identical camera and lighting settings.
- **Skin Inspector texture export:** Save full-resolution colour, alpha/finish, normal and Korea MRR inspection views as PNG files, and browse additional aircraft materials such as pilot, gunner, cockpit and attachment textures. Extracted game assets will remain local to the user's own installation.
- **Blue and green screen backgrounds:** Use clean chroma-key backgrounds for replacing the environment in image and video editors.

## Longer-term features

- **3D skin editing:** Place, move, rotate and resize artwork directly on aircraft surfaces, then include it in DDS files and Blender renders.
- **Runway scene:** Present grounded aircraft on a dedicated outdoor runway with terrain and daylight.
- **Animated turntable video export:** Create 360-degree Blender-rendered videos with configurable duration, direction and resolution, while animating supported aircraft controls and effects.
- **Player-controlled ground vehicles:** Extend PRISM's viewing, skin and rendering workflows to supported Great Battles and Tank Crew vehicles.
- **Transparent-background export:** Export the aircraft as a clean cutout, optionally with a separate shadow layer.
- **Reusable presentation presets:** Apply saved lighting, framing, background and watermark styles across different aircraft.
- **Quick Blender proof renders:** Check materials, reflections and lighting with a small Cycles preview before starting a full-resolution export.

## Already available

PRISM already includes a visual livery browser, Live DDS updates, skin inspection views, project files, aircraft posing, studio lighting, ground and sky environments, game hangars, Quick PNG exports and Blender/Cycles renders up to 8K.

Suggestions and testing feedback from IL-2 users and skin creators help shape this roadmap.
